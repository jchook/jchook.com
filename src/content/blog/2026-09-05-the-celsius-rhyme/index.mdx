> [!NOTE]
> This article is mostly helpful for us lowly Fahrenheit folks.

A little rhyme I learned in high school physics class that makes it easy to get the gist of Celsius degrees.

```
Zero's freezing
Ten is not
Twenty's warm
Thirty's hot
```

> [!NOTE]
> Ironically the word *Fahrenheit* is unmistakeably German and comes from the surname of the German-Polish-Dutch physicist Daniel Gabriel Fahrenheit (1686–1736), who proposed his temperature scale in 1724.
>
> I'm not a master of German, but *fahren* means to drive, and the *-heit* suffix is kind of like *-ness* in English — it thingafies.
>
> e.g. *sauber* → *Sauberkeit*, means "clean" → "cleanliness". So presumably, if *Fahrenheit* were a real German noun, it might mean something like "drivingness or "driveity".
